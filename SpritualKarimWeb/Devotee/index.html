Step 1

                

                  16-Digit Code Pairing & Invite Generation
                  
Every seeker generates or receives a unique 16-digit cryptographic pairing reference (e.g. SKHM-DEV4-7788-9900) or an invitation link from their guiding mentor / sponsor.

                

              

              

                
Step 2

                

                  24-Hour Cryptographic Expiry & Resend Protocol
                  
Each pairing token and link is cryptographically valid for exactly 24 hours. Seekers or mentors can resend fresh 24-hour links multiple times until official upline approval is completed.

                

              

              

                
Step 3

                

                  Personal Family Gotra Details Submission
                  
The candidate enters essential lineage data: Current Family (Spouse, Children), Husband's Family Gotra (Parents, Grandparents on Father's & Mother's sides), and Wife's Family Gotra (Parents, Grandparents).

                

              

              

                
Step 4

                

                  Instant Upline Notification & Mentor Verification
                  
Upon registration submission, the mentor receives an instant approval notification in their Pending Approvals Queue. Approving the invite links the seeker into the MLM lineage tree in closed loop.

                

              

            

          


          
          

            
            

              

                
Step 1

                

                  Basic Profile & Contact Registration
                  
Enter core identity fields: Full Name, Phone (+91 WhatsApp), Gotra, Diksha Stage, Spiritual Level, City/Location, and Designation Title.

                

              

              

                
Step 2

                

                  3-Level House Clean Governance Submission
                  
Record household energetic purification: Level 1 (Physical Clutter Removal), Level 2 (Rock Salt & Bakhoor Dhoop Purification), and Level 3 (3-Diya Sacred Energization) with room-by-room status and % completion.

                

              

              

                
Step 3

                

                  Dual-Write Persistence (Local + Firebase Cloud)
                  
Tapping Save & Sync immediately commits updates to LocalStorage and streams delta records to Firebase Realtime Database (/profiles node) with live status indication.

                

              

              

                
Step 4

                

                  Offline-First Queue Caching & Auto Reconnect Sync
                  
If disconnected, edits are securely preserved in the browser's local sync queue. When internet connectivity returns, all cached records automatically synchronize without loss of data.

                

              

            

          


          
          

            
            

              

                
Step 1

                

                  Sadhana Selection from Sacred Catalog
                  
Mentor selects an auspicious practice from the Sacred Catalog (Sri Yantra Sadhana, Kalashtami Kaal Bhairav, Navratri Chamunda, Bakhoor Sadhana, or Hanuman Sadhana) tailored to the seeker's karmic diagnostic.

                

              

              

                
Step 2

                

                  Role-Gated RBAC Authorization Matrix
                  
Only Certified Healers (Tier 2) and Admin Masters (Tier 1) hold permission to add, edit, or adjust sadhana ritual dates. Trainees and Devotees access the calendar in read-only mode.

                

              

              

                
Step 3

                

                  Healer Evaluation & Approval Sign-Off
                  
Healer inspects seeker's house clean %, daily japa mala logs, and spiritual readiness before signing off on the Sadhana Approval Form.

                

              

              

                
Step 4

                

                  Closed-Loop Verification & Live Progress Bar
                  
Once approved, the sadhana appears on the seeker's live dashboard with a live % progress bar, green pulse animation, and remaining days countdown.

                  Devotee Sadhana Application via Sadhana Explorer / Catalog

                  Instant Cryptographic Routing to Lineage Mentor

Step 3

                

                  Mentor Evaluation & Approval Sign-Off
                  
Mentor inspects devotee's household energetic purification (3 House Clean levels), gotra lineage integrity, and sadhana readiness before signing off in the Pending Approvals Queue as Trainee Sadhak (Level 3).
