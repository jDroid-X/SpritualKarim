🧘 Sadhana Drawer Enrollment & Trainee Dispatch Closed-Loop Lifecycle

Governs the interactive lifecycle when a Devotee or Mentor clicks on a spiritual practice tile to inspect details, enroll in the personal queue (btn-drawer-enroll), or transfer to Trainee In-Progress (btn-drawer-send-trainee). Enforces prerequisite validation (House Clean $\ge 75\%$), duplicate enrollment checks, quota caps (max 3 concurrent sadhanas), and multi-channel notification dispatches with closed-loop feedback.
